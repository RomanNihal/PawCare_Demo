Meet Dr. Roman Nihal

Caring for Your Pets Like Family

Dr. Roman Nihal is a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) with over 12 years of hands-on clinical experience in small animal care, surgical procedures, and preventative immunizations.

At PawCare Pro, we integrate state-of-the-art veterinary diagnosis methodologies with a warm, caring environment. Whether it's a routine booster pack vaccination or complex dental scaling under anesthesia, we prioritize your pet's comfort and long-term health.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours? +
We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
How do I book an appointment? +
Simply open the floating AI chatbot at the bottom right corner of this screen. The chatbot can view Dr. Roman's calendar, check open slots, and book you in instantly!
Is anesthesia included in dental scaling costs? +
General anesthesia is evaluated and charged separately based on your pet's size, weight, and blood work parameters to ensure maximum safety.
